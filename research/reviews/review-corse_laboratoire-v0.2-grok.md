## 1. Summary of the thesis

Corse Laboratoire is restated as a proposed territorial *application* of the Seconde Méthode and Autonomie de Capacité, not as their logical consequence. The unit of test is a bounded Act with named principals, pre-declared metrics, revers ledger, exit conditions, and a stopping rule. Capability remains multidimensional; C¹/C²/C³ are heuristic and C³ is capacitarian only under an anti-pyramid clause. The Real answers independently of the Corpus. Prior art (Sen, Sabel–Zeitlin, Teece, Hirschman, Ostrom, real options) is named as neighbour, not ancestor to be replaced. Suicide Corse is an annex. Two human arbitrations remain open: political status of the frame, and whether the annex stays.

## 2. Errors

**E1 — Inferential. §1 claims a specific additive difference from experimentalist governance that the rest of the document does not yet operationalise.**

The source says Corse Laboratoire “cherche à y ajouter notamment” an explicit effective-capability criterion, systematic bifurcation/exit, trace of Acts and revers, living mandated imputability, and transfer-of-incapacity attention. Those are design *intentions*. As of v0.2 they exist as protocols-on-paper (tables, lists), not as mechanisms shown to be absent from Sabel–Zeitlin or to survive Sabel–Zeitlin’s known failure modes (metric displacement, peer-review capture, weak-locale exclusion). Stating “we add X” before X has been run is an inferential overclaim relative to §1’s own standard (“compression opératoire située et falsifiable”). Corrected formulation: “The intended additions to experimentalist governance are listed; whether they are additions rather than restatements will be settled by Reality Tests, not by the list.”

**E2 — Inferential. §16 treats “fermeture révisable” as compatible with a stopping rule without specifying who may reopen a closed branch.**

§10 correctly refuses automatic reinterpretation of a negative result. §16 then allows provisional closure to be revised if new information appears. Without naming the reopening principal, the evidentiary bar, and a ban on the original facilitator reopening by redescription, the stopping rule of §10 can be undone by the clause of §16. Corrected formulation: “A closed branch reopens only under a named mandate, a new information criterion that is not a redescription of the failed Act, and an observer who is not the original porteur.”

No factual errors identified. No attributive errors: Sen, Nussbaum, Sabel, Zeitlin, Teece, Hirschman, Ostrom are assigned to the right families.

Claims about #1755 as documented Autonomia precedent: `[unverified: full autonomia.md § on #1755]` — not counted as error.

## 3. Novel objections

Rule N: O1–O7 of the v0.1 review are not restated. What follows is what v0.2 newly fails to hold, or holds only verbally.

**O1 — Prior-art confrontation is citation, not stress-test.**

§1 and the reference list name the neighbours requested in the first review. They do not take the neighbours’ *objections to themselves*. Sen’s capability approach is criticised in the literature for measurement underdetermination and for under-specifying collective action. Experimentalist governance is criticised for becoming a reporting machine. Dynamic capabilities are criticised as tautological (whatever surviving firms did). Hirschman warns that exit can starve voice. Those counter-arguments are the material that could still break the architecture. Naming the authors does not discharge B1–B4.

**O2 — The Act template is complete as a form and empty as a first instance.**

§10 and §15 require a bounded Act with local constraints. v0.2 still contains zero filled template. #1755 is pointed at, not instantiated as a revers ledger. A doctrine whose falsifiability depends on Acts, and which ships without one filled row, remains a method paper that talks about Reality Tests. That is internally consistent with “cadre doctrinal,” but it means H-as-family is still untested. The booster principle is stated and not used in the document that states it.

**O3 — Anti-pyramid clause for C³ is a tendency (“doit tendre à”), not a pass/fail.**

“Tendre à” lets a facilitator claim progress while dependence grows. A load-bearing anti-capture rule needs at least one hard condition (e.g. documented substitutability, or a published refusal path) rather than an aspirational list.

**O4 — Distributed principals are specified per Act; no rule addresses stacking of Acts by the same circle.**

Capture of a laboratory can proceed Act by Act, each locally well-formed, if the set of porteurs, observateurs and contradicteurs is closed. v0.2 has no rotation, recusal, or diversity constraint on the observer slot.

**O5 — “Petit commit sur le Réel” can itself become a Machine à Empêcher if smallness is used to defer any Act that would touch a real monopsony or foncier constraint.**

The booster principle is sound against architectural inflation. It is also a permission to remain in reversible trivia. The document does not say how a sequence of small Acts is prevented from systematically avoiding the local Machines à Empêcher it would have to name.
