## 1. Summary of the thesis

If freedom is the measure of effectively accessible actions, and if territorial autonomy is measured by what inhabitants can actually do rather than by the competences an institution holds, then Corsica can be treated as a laboratory that continuously runs the loop Possible → Explore → Choose → Make capable → Act → Reality → Learn → Transmit → Possible′. The draft distinguishes three machines (Prevent, Explore, Make Capable), proposes a fourth (Object), and treats Autonomie de Capacité dynamically via C¹ (act), C² (acquire new capacities), C³ (make others capable), plus dC/dt. It locates relevant futures in Possible ∩ Credible ∩ Desirable, insists that the desirable remains human, operationalizes Liberté / Égalité / Fraternité as a doctrinal reading rather than positive law, and names *Suicide Corse* as a first incarnation. The laboratory is not to prove a doctrine but to put it at risk against the Real.

## 2. Errors

**E1 — Inferential. §4 definition of Corse Laboratoire overreaches the derivation from Autonomia + Seconde Méthode.**

The source presents the passage Autonomia → Corse Laboratoire as “not a rupture” but a consequence. Autonomia supplies a *test* of institutional change (does it increase inhabitants’ effective capacities?). The Seconde Méthode supplies a *knowledge procedure*. Neither premise entails that a specific island should become the site of a continuous, territory-wide experimental loop with named machines and a project-of-society clause. That is a political choice plus a site-selection claim. Corrected formulation: “Corse Laboratoire is a proposed territorial application of Autonomie de Capacité under the Seconde Méthode; it does not follow as a necessary consequence of those two documents.”

**E2 — Inferential. §15 H is not genuinely falsifiable as written.**

H: “une organisation possibiliste et capacitaire augmente durablement l’espace des capacités effectives.” The source immediately adds distribution, robustness, dependencies, costs, bifurcation options, future effects, and effects on others. That conjunction makes almost any observed contraction interpretable as “not yet the right organisation” or “costs transferred, therefore H not tested.” A hypothesis that can absorb both expansion and contraction without specifying a stopping rule, a comparison class, a time window, or an observer independent of the project is not a Reality test. Corrected formulation: specify at least (a) a bounded Act, (b) a pre-registered capability bundle and comparison group, (c) a horizon, (d) what count of null or negative results retires H for that Act.

**E3 — Inferential. §6 treats dC/dt as if C were a scalar already defined.**

C is introduced as multidimensional (§14 explicitly refuses a single indicator). Differentiating an undefined aggregate is not a measure of “vitesse d’apprentissage capacitaire”; it is notation. The source labels this an hypothesis to examine, which is correct as status, but the formula is written as if it named an object. Corrected formulation: “No scalar C is defined; any claim about dC/dt must first specify the bundle, the aggregator M, and whose C is being timed.”

No factual or attributive errors identified in the target’s own statements about the Corpus once the “acquis / dérivation / hypothèse” labels are taken as authorial classification rather than as independently verified corpus history. Whether every “Acquis du Corpus” sentence is actually present in the cited files is `[unverified: full parallel read of all seven associated documents]`.

## 3. Novel objections

**O1 — Reification of the territory is only verbally cancelled.**

§4 says “‘La Corse est capable de X’ means a distributed configuration of persons, institutions…” That sentence is correct. The rest of the document continues to use Corse as grammatical agent of exploring, choosing, learning, transmitting, and revising a projet de société (§§4, 12, 17, conclusion). The verbal disclaimer does not bind the political use. A laboratory that “puts the doctrine in danger” still needs named principals who can be held to account when the Real objects. Without that, capture of the laboratory by the authorial circle is not an external risk; it is the default organisational form.

**O2 — Possible ∩ Crédible ∩ Désirable collapses three different kinds of constraint into a Venn region.**

Possible (physical / logical), Credible (resource, law, cost, dependence), Desirable (those who bear consequences) are not three sets of the same type. Intersection notation suggests a residual zone waiting to be occupied. It hides: (i) conflict among desirables; (ii) strategic misrepresentation of credibility; (iii) path-dependence of what becomes possible *after* an Act. The sovereignty clause (“IA éclaire; les vivants gouvernent”) is already in the Seconde Méthode and does not solve intra-living conflict over the desirable.

**O3 — C³ (“rendre capable de rendre capable”) has no anti-pyramid rule.**

Recursion of enablement is also how patronage, clerical capture, platform lock-in, and missionary states describe themselves. The source’s later non-capture and sortie tests do not yet constrain C³. Without a requirement that the newly capable agent can refuse, substitute, or teach a rival method, C³ describes accumulation of disciples.

**O4 — Machine à Objecter has no operational owner, trigger, or stopping rule distinct from Reviewer-as-role.**

Cogentia already separates Redactor and Reviewer. Naming a “machine” adds ontology without adding a mechanism that the existing role does not already claim. The source itself warns that objection can become a Machine à Empêcher. That warning is a concession (see §4). What is *not* conceded: who may silence an objection, who may override delay, and whether the Objecter may act on Acts already launched. Absent those, the fourth machine is either decorative or a new veto point.

**O5 — Test de sortie (§11) is specified as a thought experiment after withdrawal, not as an institutional right.**

“Après retrait ou remplacement du dispositif, quelles capacités demeurent ?” is an analyst’s question. A non-capture criterion that cannot be exercised by the dependent party without the facilitator’s cooperation is not a sortie test; it is an autopsy. Hirschman’s exit is a capability of the user, not of the evaluator.

**O6 — Republican operationalization (§13) is framed correctly as non-positive-law, but still occupies the names of constitutional principles.**

The source says it does not replace juridical meanings. Using the three words as capacity-metrics in a Corsican laboratory will be read, in French public debate, as a claim on the Republic’s language. That is a political fact, not a doctrinal footnote. The document does not examine that reception risk.

**O7 — Falsification is assigned to “the Real” while the observer remains the Corpus.**

§15: if devices produce bureaucracy, capture, fragility, “le Réel aura objecté” and “l’objection devra entrer dans le Corpus.” The Corpus is authored by the same circle that designed the laboratory. An objection that must be ingested by the project in order to count is not an independent Reality test.

## 7. Fragile or ambiguous concepts

- **Machine à Rendre Capable.** Defined as conversion-factor assembly (information, skill, tool, access, resource, coordination, finance, time, relation, infrastructure). That list is Sen’s conversion factors plus coordination. What would stabilize: one worked Corsican conversion (e.g. a right that exists and an action that remains inaccessible), naming the missing factor and the Act that supplies it.
- **Machine à Objecter.** Role-name without mechanism. Stabilize by mapping onto existing Reviewer contract + a public objection register with time-boxed response, or drop the machine noun.
- **C¹ / C² / C³.** Metaphorically clear; extensionally overlapping (teaching is both C² and C³). Stabilize with examples and a refusal/substitution clause for C³.
- **dC/dt.** See E3.
- **Valeur de bifurcation.** Real-options language without an option-exercise cost. Stabilize by distinguishing option value from slack / unused redundancy.
- **Réversibilité / révisabilité.** “S’engager sans rendre la révision impossible” is a maxim, not a test. Stabilize with one irreversible Act the doctrine is willing to recommend and one revision channel that would remain.
- **Test de sortie.** See O5. Stabilize as a user-exercisable right, or rename as *ex post* audit.
- **Corse Laboratoire.** Vacillates between method, place, project of society, and interface for Suicide Corse. Stabilize by picking a primary referent per section.

## 8A. Possibility-space closure / Booster test

- **Present-state invariant.** The document treats “territory as the unit of capability accounting” as given. That excludes non-territorial capability communities (diaspora, professional orders, platforms, energy offtakers) that already structure Corsican action-spaces. It also treats “inhabitants” as the relevant demos without examining seasonal residents, property-absent owners, or State agents who live the island.
- **Impossibility-status error.** None material. The text is careful not to call current closures impossible.
- **Unassimilated residue.** Corsican high-proximity social structure (clans, honour, suspicion cycles already theorised by the author elsewhere as DRSJ) appears only as generic “habitants.” A laboratory on this island that does not model proximity-induced objection-as-veto is missing its own site. The residue was normalized into “démocratie capable.”
- **Booster opportunity.** Before building four machines and a societal project, the smallest reversible Act consistent with the doctrine is already named in Autonomia practice: a public, bounded test of one conversion factor (the #1755 capacity test is the Corpus precedent). Running one such Act and publishing the revers ledger would test more of H than another doctrinal compression. Candidate Reality test: pick one existing inaccessible action in Haute-Corse, supply one missing conversion factor, pre-register the sortie condition, stop at a date.

## 8B. Unexplored-space / blind-spot review

**B1 — Capability approach prior art as conversion-factor theory, not just as slogan.**
Sen’s conversion factors and Nussbaum’s list are the nearest academic architecture to “Machine à Rendre Capable.” The draft rediscovers conversion without stating what it adds (trace, Act, anti-capture, territorial recursion) and what it refuses (a fixed list of central capabilities). Material because novelty and architecture claims depend on the difference. Smallest check: a one-page contrast table.

**B2 — Experimentalist governance (Sabel–Zeitlin) and democratic experimentalism (Dorf–Sabel).**
The laboratory loop is functionally the experimentalist cycle: framework goals → local experiment → peer review → revision of goals. The draft does not confront the known failure modes of experimentalism (goal displacement onto metrics, peer-review capture, exclusion of weak locales). Material because §12–15 claim a democratic laboratory. Smallest check: map loop steps onto Sabel’s four functions and mark absences.

**B3 — Dynamic capabilities and real / switching options.**
C² and “valeur de bifurcation” are close to Teece (sensing/seizing/transforming) and to option-to-switch. The draft’s advance would be political imputability and anti-capture. That advance is not argued against this literature. Smallest check: say whether C² is a territorial dynamic capability or something else.

**B4 — Exit as user capability (Hirschman), not only as analyst’s sortie.**
See O5. Material for §11. Smallest check: rewrite sortie as an exercisable alternative, or explain why voice-without-exit is enough here.

**B5 — Operational Corsican constraints as experimental material.**
Foncier, cadastral opacity, energy monopsony, seasonal demography, EU State-aid, status negotiations, municipal fragmentation are absent. A laboratory that never names the local Machines à Empêcher it will actually meet is exploring a generic island. Smallest check: one annex listing candidate Acts already blocked, with the blocking factor.

**B6 — Commons governance failure modes (Ostrom).**
C³ and shared infrastructure are commons problems. Design principles (boundaries, monitoring, graduated sanctions, nested enterprise) are unexplored. Material for “rendre capable de rendre capable.”
