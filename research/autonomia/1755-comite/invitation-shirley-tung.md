# Draft — not sent

**To:** Shirley F. Tung <sftung@ksu.edu>  
**Subject:** James Boswell, Pascal Paoli and the reception of Corsican constitutional history — scholarly inquiry

Dear Professor Tung,

I am writing on behalf of the Institut Mariani in Corsica, where we are developing an open, source-based research project on the Corsican constitutional experience of 1729–1769, particularly the institutions associated with Pascal Paoli in 1755.

Your UCLA dissertation, *A Self-Reflexive Journey*, and your article on Boswell as an “isthmus” between Paoli and Johnson are directly relevant to one of our questions: how did the Corsican cause circulate through British publishing, travel writing and public debate, and where should one draw the line between cultural reception and demonstrable constitutional influence?

The project's documentary working paper is public:
https://github.com/JeanHuguesRobert/barons-Mariani/blob/main/research/autonomia/projet_1755.md

We are exploring the formation of a small, independent, international scholarly group. Its purpose would be to critically examine evidence and competing interpretations, not to endorse any predetermined historical or political conclusion.

At this preliminary stage, may I ask whether you could suggest essential sources, scholars or objections that our inquiry should not overlook? If the project falls within your interests, I would also welcome your view on what a useful and appropriately limited scholarly contribution might look like. There is no assumption or expectation of a commitment to the proposed group.

Thank you for considering this inquiry.

Yours sincerely,

Jean Hugues Noël Robert  
Institut Mariani / C.O.R.S.I.C.A.  
Corte, Corsica, France
