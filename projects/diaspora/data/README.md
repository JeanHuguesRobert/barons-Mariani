# Seed

`seed.json` is the phase-1 public dataset. Schema id: `diaspora.seed.v0`.

Corsica Diaspora has two public addresses, kept apart:

- legal seat, from the Recherche d'entreprises API on 2026-09-30: BAT B 4 RUE BELVEDERE RES ORNANO 20200 BASTIA, with that response's coordinates;
- correspondence address printed on https://www.corsica-diaspora.com/ : 11 rue Saint-Joseph, 20200 Bastia, not geocoded here.

The IBAN printed on that website is not in the seed.

Amicale Corsica is localized at Eysines from the municipal directory. The street, the named officer, the mobile number, and the personal email on that page are not in the seed. The municipal site link `http://sucorsu.free.fr` did not answer on 2026-09-30 and is not offered as a live website.

C.O.R.S.I.C.A. and Institut Mariani are corpus statements. Their Corte coordinates are an approximate city centroid, not a rooftop fix. No current RNA extract for C.O.R.S.I.C.A. was read in this pass.
