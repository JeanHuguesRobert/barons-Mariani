# Digipees — history and provenance

Digipees originated in the d2lab project in the mid-2000s as a visual family associated with the idea of Digital People.

Historical source SVG files recovered from archived email exchanges are to be preserved before normalization or extension work.

## Current preservation targets

- `digipees21-08-2006.svg`
- `digipies02.svg`

Historical originals should remain unchanged. Cleaned, normalized, colorized or generated derivatives should live separately and retain explicit provenance back to their source assets.
