# Digipees — provenance and rights

Related issue: [barons-Mariani #34](https://github.com/JeanHuguesRobert/barons-Mariani/issues/34).
Companion documents: `../originals/PROVENANCE.md` (file-level provenance of the two
recovered SVGs), `../HISTORY.md`, `../../research/generative_graphic_design_lineage.md`.

This note records who was involved in releasing the d2lab Digipees graphical assets, on
what terms, and what remains open before any broad relaunch or trademark filing. It is a
rights record, not a legal opinion; email addresses of the individuals named below are
withheld from this public document by design, even though their names are given.

## The October 2007 release agreement

On 2007-10-07, Jean Hugues Robert proposed, by email, releasing d2lab's intellectual,
artistic and other intangible assets — explicitly including Yvan Balsamo's graphics,
website code and ideas — using wording referring to **"public domain"** and **MPL 1.1**.

Four people replied the same evening and the following morning, each approving:

| Date/time | Person | Reply (original) | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2007-10-07 12:43 | Thierry Parmentelat | "personnellement j'y suis favorable" | personally, I am in favor |
| 2007-10-07 16:58 | Jean-François Catz | "Ok pour moi, excellente idee" | OK for me, excellent idea |
| 2007-10-07 17:10 | Yvan Balsamo | "Idem pour moi… Savoir mon boulot continuer de vivre, quelle magnifique occasion. Avec plaisir !!!!" | Same for me; knowing my work can continue to live is a wonderful opportunity. With pleasure. |
| 2007-10-07 21:19 | Yvan Balsamo | "Impeccable pour moi aussi… Avec plaisir…" | Perfectly fine for me too; with pleasure. |
| 2007-10-08 00:44 | Rodolphe Briet | "C'est bon pour moi." | It's fine by me / I agree. |

Source: Jean Hugues Robert's personal email archive (search conducted 2026-08-13/14).
Quotes are reproduced from that archive; the emails themselves are not published here.

### Roles

- **Yvan Balsamo** — the creator of the Digipees graphics (and other d2lab graphical/web
  work). His own words above are the strongest evidence of intent: he was glad for the
  work to "continue to live."
- **Thierry Parmentelat** and **Jean-François Catz** — among the original d2lab owners
  prior to cession, approving the release.
- **Rodolphe Briet** — recipient of the later cession of d2lab; also approved the release
  in the same thread.
- **Jean-François Catz** is separately the person from whom the two surviving SVG sources
  were recovered in 2018 (see `../originals/PROVENANCE.md`).

## The "public domain" / MPL 1.1 ambiguity

The original 2007 proposal named two different things in the same breath: **"public
domain"** (no rights reserved at all) and **MPL 1.1** (the Mozilla Public License 1.1, a
copyleft software license with attribution and same-license-on-modified-files terms).
These are not equivalent, and MPL 1.1 is a software license applied loosely here to
artistic/graphical assets. Nothing in the surviving replies resolves which of the two was
meant, or whether the distinction was considered at the time. This is recorded as an
**open historical ambiguity**, not resolved retroactively by interpretation.

## Present licensing decision

Independently of how the 2007 wording is read, Jean Hugues Robert — as the person to whom
release was confirmed by all parties with a stake in the assets — has decided the
operative license going forward for the Digipees graphical assets hosted in this
repository:

> **CC BY-SA 4.0** — [creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.en)

This applies to the historical originals under `../originals/` and to derived/normalized
work under `../normalized/` and elsewhere in this hosted repository, unless a more
specific notice says otherwise. See `../LICENSE.md`.

## Naming/trademark clearance checklist

Before any trademark filing or broad public relaunch under the name "Digipees" or
"Digital People":

- [x] Search EU trademark register (EUIPO eSearch plus) for "digipees" — **0 results**
      across Trade marks, Designs, Owners and Representatives (checked 2026-08-14,
      direct query against `euipo.europa.eu/eSearch`). No collision found.
- [x] Search EU trademark register (EUIPO eSearch plus) for "digital people" —
      **3 registered EUTMs found**, all unrelated to this project, and two of them
      directly on point:
      - `017258864` — figurative mark **"Digital People"**, owner name **"Digital
        People"**, Nice classes **9, 38, 42, 45** (computer software; telecoms; tech/
        design services; personal/legal services), registered 2019-07-10. A real company
        trading under this exact name, in overlapping classes.
      - `W01547378` — word mark **"DIGITAL PEOPLE"**, owner **Soul Machines Limited**,
        Nice class 42, registered 2020-04-21 (international registration designating
        the EU).
      - `018932374` — **"TALEA GROUP DIGITAL PEOPLE ORIENTED"**, a compound mark, lower
        collision risk but same words present.
      → **"Digital People" is not clear** for a software/design relaunch in the EU.
      **"Digipees" is clear** at the EU trademark level.
- [x] Check domain name availability for `digipees.com`, `digipees.fr`, `digipees.org` —
      **none resolve** (DNS lookup failure on all three, checked 2026-08-14), consistent
      with the name being unused/available. `.net` and others not checked.
- [x] Check for existing commercial products/services using "Digipees" as a brand — no
      exact match found in general web search; only phonetically close but unrelated
      names exist (DigiPe — Indian fintech; Digipeeks — YouTube channel; Digipepe — a
      meme coin), none of which use the "Digipees" spelling or operate in an adjacent
      field.
- [ ] Search French trademark register (INPI) for "Digipees" and "Digital People" —
      **attempted, blocked**: `data.inpi.fr` returns a Cloudflare bot-protection page to
      automated/headless access (checked 2026-08-14). Needs a manual search by a human
      with an ordinary browser at
      [data.inpi.fr](https://data.inpi.fr/search?q=digipees&type=brands) before any
      French filing decision — the EUIPO result above does not substitute for it (a
      purely French national mark would not necessarily show up in the EU register).
- [ ] Confirm with Yvan Balsamo whether "Digipees" was his own naming or d2lab's, and
      whether he has any separate naming preference for a 2026 relaunch.
- [ ] Decide whether the FractaVolta-themed / Corsica-specific variant should carry a
      distinct name (e.g. `digipees-corsica`, per
      `../../research/generative_graphic_design_lineage.md` §11) to reduce naming-rights
      exposure on the historical name itself.

**Working conclusion:** nothing found blocks continued use of **"Digipees"** specifically.
Avoid leaning on the generic label **"Digital People"** as a standalone brand/product name
in the EU — an unrelated company already holds it in the relevant classes. The two
remaining items are calls for Jean Hugues Robert / Yvan Balsamo, not further research.
